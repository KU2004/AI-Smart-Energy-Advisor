# ⚡ AI Smart Energy Advisor

## 🚀 Overview
AI Smart Energy Advisor is an interactive dashboard that helps users understand their electricity usage and get simple recommendations to save energy and reduce costs.

It uses machine learning on real-world energy data to provide insights, predictions, and decision support in a clean and easy-to-use interface.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement
Most users do not understand their energy consumption patterns, leading to higher electricity bills and inefficient usage.

This project solves that by:
- Analyzing past energy data
- Predicting future usage
- Providing actionable suggestions

---

## 💡 Features

### 📊 Energy Overview
- Average, highest, and lowest usage
- Clear visualization of usage trends

### 📈 Forecasting
- Predicts future energy usage using AI models

### 💡 Smart Recommendations
- Personalized suggestions to reduce electricity usage
- Easy-to-understand (non-technical)

### ⏰ Best Time Detection
- Suggests best and worst times to use electricity

### 🧠 Decision Helper
- Helps users decide when to use appliances

### 🚨 Alert System
- Warns users about high energy usage

### 📅 Daily Energy Plan
- Simple guide for daily electricity usage

### 📥 Download Report
- Generates a simple energy report
